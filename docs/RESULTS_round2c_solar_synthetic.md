## 2. Strengths of ReuseGate

1. **Cyclic access larger than the cache (round-robin over m > K items).** FIFO and LRU fall to about 0. ReuseGate keeps a stable subset and scores 0.45 (m=20), 0.30 (m=30), 0.18 (m=50),
   close to the K/m share of a frozen cache (0.50, 0.33, 0.20), and above SOLAR-A, SOLAR and LFU at every m from 15 to 50 (the seed-to-seed sd is 0 at m ≥ 20, so these gaps are real for this simulator). At m=10 the three are within noise.
2. **It admits much less than the always-admit baselines** (admission rate 0.02 to 0.12 in cycling), but SOLAR-A and SOLAR admit about as little (0.10 to 0.12), so this is not a separate advantage.
3. It needs no feedback labels and no per-answer quality score (SOLAR uses one).

## 3. Weaknesses of ReuseGate (several were predicted in the pre-registration; the size of the drift failure was not)

1. **Topic drift: severe.** Hit rate 0.19 against 0.95 for FIFO, at every phase length tested (50 to 500 steps) and every overlap; after a phase boundary it does not recover (0.043 in the 20 steps after a boundary, 0.055 in the rest of the phase).
   SOLAR-A and SOLAR are also below FIFO here (0.68 and 0.62 at 100-step phases; SOLAR-A reaches 0.93 only for 500-step phases), so SOLAR is not immune, but ReuseGate is far worse.
   A plausible reason (not isolated by an experiment) is that its score has no recency term, so residents with a large count from an earlier topic outrank newcomers. The exploratory aging period of 100 queries
   raises the 100-step-phase hit rate only from 0.19 to 0.32 (ReuseGate) and from 0.37 to 0.49 (ReuseGate-E); FIFO is at 0.95, so the aging constant is not the whole explanation.
2. **Working-set sweep: below FIFO for caches smaller than the working set.** With the pre-registered cosine radius 0.5, ReuseGate is up to 0.16 below FIFO under uniform popularity (K=30; ReuseGate-E 0.18) and up to 0.23 below under Zipf (K=10); it matches FIFO only for K ≥ 150.
   SOLAR-A and SOLAR beat FIFO by 0.08 to 0.20 at K = 10 to 20 under uniform popularity and by 0.02 to 0.09 under Zipf at K ≤ 30, but fall 0.02 (uniform) and 0.07 (Zipf) **below** FIFO for large caches
   because their admission gate stays closed (a stuck gate; ReuseGate does not show this at K ≥ 150).
3. **The radius matters (sensitivity, labelled).** With radius 0.7 ReuseGate reaches 0.777 (K=20, FIFO 0.727), 0.970 (K=50, FIFO 0.944) and 0.653 (Zipf K=10, FIFO 0.677); with radius 0.3 it is similar to 0.5.
   So the working-set deficit above is mostly the radius guess for this embedding space, not the design; the radius would have to be set from reference traffic (our real streams do this) and the SOLAR synthetic runs offer no such traffic. Drift is unaffected by the radius (0.18 to 0.20).
4. **No guarantee.** SOLAR proves a constant competitive ratio under bounded stale advantage and a sublinear regret bound under stationary utilities; ReuseGate has none, and the drift result shows what a missing non-stationarity argument costs.
5. **Retrieval-noise stream: no separation.** All policies are within 0.01 hit rate and the seed sd is about 0.02, so nothing here supports or contradicts either approach.

## 4. What this means for the paper

* Do **not** claim that ReuseGate beats SOLAR in general. The honest statement: in our cache streams (stationary-per-window, labelled traffic, reference budget) ReuseGate and ReuseGate-R beat the SOLAR variants (Stage A/B); on LoCoMo SOLAR is better; in synthetic stress tests ReuseGate wins under cyclic scans larger than the cache and
  loses badly under topic drift, and loses under small caches unless its radius is calibrated.
* We did not test our real-stream windows for topic drift, so the paper cannot say whether the drift weakness appears there; it should list it as a limitation: a production stream with a sudden topic shift may need a recency or aging component.
* Two directions suggested by the data, not tested here: (i) combine the demand score with a recency term or reset it at detected drift; (ii) tune the radius on reference traffic (already how ReuseGate is configured in the cache streams).

## 5. Limits of this evidence

Synthetic embeddings (Gaussian topic clusters, dim 128); 3 seeds; the ReuseGate parameters were fixed before the run and the radius guess was poor for this space; the period-100 variant is exploratory and was chosen after seeing the failure;
no numerical comparison with the paper's own tables; AUTO/SCION not run.
